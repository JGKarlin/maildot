# Privacy

Hooner LLC publishes Mail Dot. This page states what Mail Dot does with mail and with data.

## What Mail Dot collects

Mail Dot collects nothing. It has no server of its own. It contains no tracking, analytics or advertising, and it sends nothing to Hooner LLC or to its developer.

## Where mail goes

Mail Dot connects Apple Mail on the Mac to Dot in the user's own ChatGPT account. The text of a message goes to ChatGPT in three cases: when the user shares a message with Dot, when Mail Dot offers an important message to Dot for a draft, and when Dot looks a message up to answer a question the user asked. Automatic drafts can be switched off for any account.

OpenAI's terms and privacy policy govern what ChatGPT receives. The connection between the Mac and ChatGPT is created in the user's own OpenAI account and carries only that user's requests.

## What stays on the Mac

Mail Dot keeps its settings, Dot's drafts and reports, and a record of its own actions in the user's macOS account. The Mac's login and disk encryption protect this data; Mail Dot does not encrypt it separately. The key that runs the connection is kept in the macOS Keychain.

## Sending mail

Mail Dot never sends a message by itself. A draft is sent only after the user confirms it on the Mac.

## Removing data

`Tools/Remove Mail Dot.command` removes Mail Dot and its stored data from the Mac. The plugin and the scheduled task in ChatGPT are deleted in ChatGPT.

## Questions

Questions belong on the project's Issues page on GitHub. Reports should not include the text of real mail.
