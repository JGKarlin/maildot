Welcome to Mail Dot

Mail Dot lets Dot, your ChatGPT assistant, read and draft replies to your mail inside Apple Mail.

This installer puts Mail Dot in your Applications folder. Afterwards Mail Dot opens and walks you through switching it on and connecting it to Dot. That takes about five minutes.

Before you install: quit Mail. If Mail is open, the installer asks you to quit it. You also need the ChatGPT app on this Mac, signed in with a plan that includes Dot.
